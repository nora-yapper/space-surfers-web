Setup

How we demo it in class

The real Space Surfers is played standing on a board in front of a big screen. A Kinect v2 camera under the screen watches the rider, and the game reads how they lean. This page shows how we set it up for our class demo: the room, once, and a short calibration for every rider.

What you need

Camera

A Kinect v2 with its adapter. It needs a USB 3 port and its own power plug.

Computer

A computer running our tracker and the game.

Screen

A projector or large screen.

Board

A board sticker on the floor. The tracker watches a 1.4 by 1.0 m area around it. Riders 160 cm and up.

1. Set up the room

Once per room, by whoever runs it

Step by step

  1. Place the camera under the screen, about 80 cm off the floor, facing straight ahead with no tilt. Plug it into a USB 3 port and into its power plug.
  2. Stick the board on the floor straight in front of the camera, long side pointing at the screen, with its centre about 3 m away (2.3 to 3.6 m). Keep the space around it clear. A dim room is fine, because the camera sees in infrared. Keep direct sunlight off the camera.
  3. Start the tracker. Its window shows what the camera sees, plus the camera height and how far away the board is.
  4. Record the empty room. Press B. You have 8 seconds to leave the camera's view, so one person can do this alone. Everyone stays out of view until it says it is done.
  5. Mark the board. Press P, then stand on the sticker within 8 seconds. The outline of the tracked area moves under your feet.
  6. Check it. The tracker should show a camera height of about 0.80 m and a board distance between 2.3 and 3.6 m. If it tells you to move the board, move it and press P again. If the outline floats above the floor or sinks into it, [ and ] lower or raise the floor by 2 cm.

The setup is saved. Next time, only repeat it if the camera, the board or the furniture moved.

2. Each rider

Before every ride, guided by the game, under a minute

When someone steps on the board, the game walks them through it on screen. Nobody needs to touch the computer. Directions always mean the screen, not the body, because the rider stands sideways.

On screenWhat the rider does
Welcome, riderSteps on the board
Stand sidewaysTurns left or right like on a surfboard, feet and shoulders sideways to the screen
You steer by leaningLearns that their biggest safe lean becomes a full turn, and that directions mean the screen
Stand still and relaxedFeet on the board, arms down. The game records their neutral stance and height
Lean toward the screenMoves the blue dot into the arrow and holds it for a second. This makes the board dive
Lean away from the screenSame, to make the board climb
Lean to the screen's left, then rightSame, to steer. After each lean they stand straight again
Now try itMoves the dot freely. This is exactly how the board will move

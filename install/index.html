Install

Playing with your body

The real Space Surfers is played standing on a board in front of a big screen. A Kinect v2 camera watches you from the front and the game reads how you lean.

What you need

Camera

A Kinect v2 with its USB adapter, facing the board.

Computer

A Mac running our tracker and the Godot build of the game.

Screen

A projector or large screen in front of the rider.

Board

A floor board inside a 1.4 by 1.0 m zone the camera can see. Riders 160 cm and up.

Running it

  1. Start the tracker. It reads the infrared camera, finds your shoulders, hips and hands, and sends your lean to the game.
  2. Start the game. It waits on the start screen until someone steps on the board.
  3. Calibrate. The game first has each new rider stand sideways on the board, then walks them through each lean once with a live meter. It takes about a minute.
  4. Ride. Lean to steer, hold a lean to confirm, raise both hands to restart, step off to pause.

Dark clothing can hide from the depth sensor, so the tracker works from the infrared image. A download kit with the tracker is not ready yet. Until then, the browser version plays the same game with the arrow keys.

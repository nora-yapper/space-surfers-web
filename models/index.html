Models

What the city is built from

Every model in Space Surfers, rendered straight from the game files in Godot. The board, boost pad, catwalk, neon signs and tiger crate are made by our team. The rest of the city is open-licensed models, placed by the song. Every author is also credited in the game (press C on the start screen).

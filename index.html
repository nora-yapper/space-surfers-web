SPACE SURFERS

Chase the orb through the hanging city

You stand on a real board and lean to steer a hoverboard down a trail made from a song. Stay on the trail and the music plays clean while you close in on the orb. Drift off and the song bends, slows and breaks up, and your power drains.

Play in the browser Keyboard version. Chrome or Firefox on a laptop or desktop.

This is the keyboard version

Space Surfers is made to be played with your body: you stand on a board in front of a Kinect v2 infrared camera, and the game reads how you lean. That needs the camera, a Mac, and our tracker software, so it only runs in our installation for now.

The browser version is the same game with the arrow keys standing in for your body. It shows the whole flow, from stepping on the board to catching the orb, but it can't give you the feeling of leaning to ride.

How a ride goes

  1. Step on the board. In the browser, press Enter.
  2. Pick a difficulty. Easy, Medium or Hard: the same song, but a busier city and less help staying on the trail.
  3. Get ready, then 3, 2, 1. The orb breaks loose and flies ahead along the trail.
  4. Follow the trail. Staying on it brings the orb closer. Drifting off, or hitting an obstacle, pushes it away and drains your power.
  5. Catch the orb before the song ends, or make it to the end with power left. Run out of power and the ride is over.

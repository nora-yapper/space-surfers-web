Sound

The song is the level

Nothing in a ride is placed by hand. Before the game runs, we analyse the song offline: how loud it is, how bright it sounds, where the beats fall. The trail, the city and the obstacles are all built from that.

Song to world

In the songIn the game
LoudnessHow high the trail rides, and how tall and busy the city gets
Brightness (timbre)How far the trail bends left and right
KickCatwalks across the trail: dive under
SnarePipe bundles across the trail: rise over
Hi-hatCargo haulers parked against the walls
Bar startsContainers swinging from cranes
VocalsNeon signs sticking out from the walls
The whole songHaze thickens and the flying space slowly climbs, so you rise away from the ground

Harder songs add more sound layers as obstacles and space them closer. The sounds are estimated from frequency bands, without separating instruments.

Body to sound

On the trail

The song plays clean and you close in on the orb.

Off the trail

The song distorts and your power drains. Steer back and it comes back.

Hit something

No crash. You lose speed and the orb pulls away.

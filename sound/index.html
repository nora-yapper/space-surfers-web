Sound

The song is the level

Nothing in a ride is placed by hand. Before the game runs, we analyse the song offline: how loud it is, how bright it sounds, where the beats fall. The trail, the city and the obstacles are all built from that.

Song to world

In the songIn the game
Loudness
How strong the sound is at each moment. Quiet verses, loud choruses and drops.		How high the trail rides, and how tall and busy the city gets
Brightness (timbre)
Whether the sound is dark and deep, or sharp and sparkly. Lots of high notes and cymbals sound bright; bass and pads sound dark.		How far the trail bends left and right
Kick
The big low drum, the "boom" you feel in your chest. In dance music it usually hits on every beat.		Catwalks across the trail: dive under
Snare
The sharp "crack" drum, usually on the second and fourth beat. It is what you clap along to.		Pipe bundles across the trail: rise over
Hi-hat
The small, fast "tss tss" cymbal that keeps the rhythm ticking between the drums.		Trams hanging from cables across the street
Bar starts
Music is counted in groups of four beats, called bars. "One, two, three, four, one..." This is every "one".		Containers swinging from cranes
Lead melody
The main tune you could hum along to, played by a singer or a lead synth.		Neon signs sticking out from the walls
The whole song
From the first second to the last, how far along you are.		Haze thickens and the flying space slowly climbs, so you rise away from the ground

Each difficulty has its own song, and so its own trail and city: Easy rides "Synthwave", Medium "Stranger" and Hard "Hard Cyberpunk". Harder difficulties also add more sound layers as obstacles and space them closer. The sounds are estimated from frequency bands, without separating instruments, so "lead melody" means sustained melody in the voice range.

Body to sound

The song tells you where the trail is, so you can ride by ear. All five versions of the song play in sync, each shifted in pitch, and the game blends between them as you move.

What you doWhat you hear
Ride on the trailThe song plays clean, and you close in on the orb
Drift above the trailThe pitch bends up, up to two semitones
Drift below the trailThe pitch bends down
Drift leftThe song sounds muffled and moves to the right, toward the trail
Drift rightThe song sounds thin and moves to the left, toward the trail
Drift far offThe song saturates and slows down, and your power drains
Ride over a boost padThe song speeds up 1.5 times for 5 seconds, tape style, and the city rushes by with it

Made in code

Two of the sounds are not files: the game builds them from sine tones when it starts. Listen to them here.

Rewarding chime

Two quick rising notes, E6 then B6. Plays when you clear an obstacle and when a calibration lean is reached.

Countdown

A beep (A5) on each of 3, 2 and 1, then a higher, longer one (A6) to go.

Menu music

Everything that is not the ride has its own looping music: idle, onboarding, the song picker, the get ready screen, pause and the end screens. It fades out over the 3, 2, 1 count, so in the ride you only hear the song, and it comes back after.
